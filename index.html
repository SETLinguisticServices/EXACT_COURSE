Estructura del Curso por Sesiones

Diseño curricular enfocado en deconstrucción y estrategias del examen.

Reading & Writing

Sessions 01 - 10
  • 01/02: Reading Part 1 presented & tasks given.
  • 03/04: Reading Part 2 reinforcement & tasks.
  • 05/06: Reading Part 3 presented & tasks given.
  • 07/08: Reading Part 4 presented & tasks given.
  • 09/10: Reading Part 5 presented & writing focus.

Listening

Sessions 11 - 14
  • 11: Listening Part 1 presented & tasks.
  • 12: Listening Part 2 reinforcement & tasks.
  • 13: Listening Part 3 presented & tasks.
  • 14: Listening Part 4 presented & tasks.

Speaking

Sessions 15 - 18
  • 15: Speaking Part 1 presented & tasks.
  • 16: Speaking Part 2 reinforcement & tasks.
  • 17: Speaking Part 3 presented & tasks.
  • 18: Speaking Practice & Formative Assessment.

Final Evaluation

Sessions 19 - 20
  • 19: Full Mock Exam Practice (1.5 Hours).
  • 20: Diagnostic Feedback & Strategic Closure.
